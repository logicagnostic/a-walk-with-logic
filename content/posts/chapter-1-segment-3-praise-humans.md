INT. NONDESCRIPT BUILDING, 4:34 PM

*Logic and Agnostic settle near the wall, blending into the edges of the crowd's attention. The band holds their place at the back of the stage, instruments ready, underscoring the room with quiet expectancy. At the front, Deacon Denial stands firm at the pulpit. The room is alive, every eye forward, focused, waiting for the first words of our sermon.*

**Deacon Denial**

Welcome to another sermon on the AGNOSTIC mount. I'm Brother Deacon Denial and today's message is a humanist one. *[The organ trills between Deacon's words]*

Every day I hear someone thank god for something made by the hands of a human. Someone praises god for the miracles of a human.

A cancer is cured. A meal is prepared a life is made spared or brought back. And we say Praise be to god.

But if there is a being so powerful, so all-encompassing, that it played a role in every minute of life. Had a finger in every aspect of our lives…

Do you really think that it would be so vain to need constant praise to be validated? Would a being so mighty, be so weak-willed to require eight billion ants called humans to always bend at the knee in praise?

**Crowd**

Nooooo! *[Loudly, together]*

**Deacon Denial**

*[The full band kicks into a driving beat]*

Let me ask it another way. Wouldn't it be more valuable if we gave that same constant praise to each other?

*[The crowd cheers]*

Say, for the food we eat, rather than heaping praise on a deity that we can't agree upon. We praised the people that made it directly.

**Crowd**

Hallelujah! *[Loudly, together]*

**Deacon Denial**

Wouldn't it be more beneficial to give all of that praise to those people?

**Crowd**

Yes! *[Loudly, together]*

**Deacon Denial**

How much value do you think that would add to human-kind?

*[The crowd cheers]*

My question is simple. How much better would we feel to receive that praise instead of judgment?

**Crowd**

Hallelujah! *[Loudly, together]*

**Deacon Denial**

Just for a moment. Don't think of this as a violation of a god. Ask yourself how good it would feel to be praised just as we do our gods. If this pup, Logic was able to speak and every time you did something even minutely for her she said.

*[Logic barks acknowledgement]*

"Oh, mama I love you. Praise, you. You are the best." She constantly heaps praise. How good would that feel right here in this place you call a heart?

Now amplify that by 8 billion good feelings and we briefly slow human suffering. We give everyone a moment of Love. Am I right?

**Crowd**

Yes! *[Loudly, together]*

**Deacon Denial**

Love is just another word for praise, for thanks.

How much better off do you think we'd be if we stopped praising the air, the sun, or a mythical being in the sky? Instead, we gave praise to the humans, both near and far…

*[The crowd is joyfully hyped]*

Praise humans. Praise humans. Thank you, I praise you. Amen. A-woman and a human-kind.

*[The cheers increase]*

**Logic**

How are these people not hearing me? I can talk f\*\*\*ers.

**Deacon Denial**

Praise be to humans. Praise be to humans. Praise be to humans. Now that's the motto, I can get behind.

**Logic**

It's definitely got a ring to it. Although it forgets the rest of us animals.

**Agnostic**

Come on, girl. It's time to catch up with Caldwell.
