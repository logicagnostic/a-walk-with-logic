EXT. SIDE STREET — MID-AFTERNOON

*The sound kicks in first; that familiar whistle, half showdown, half playground taunt. A plastic bag rolls across the road, standing in for the tumbleweed that never made it to the suburbs.*

*Two dogs lock eyes from opposite curbs. One is Logic, ears forward, stance certain. The other, Frank, is all bark and borrowed confidence wrapped in a hypoallergenic labradoodle. The world seems to hold its breath.*

*(Low growl builds. A single bark breaks the silence and the standoff begins.)*

**Neighbor**

Settle, settle.

**Agnostic**

Logic, settle down. That dog is 13 times your size.

*[The hu-mans attempt to control.]*

**Logic**

Hey, Frank.

**Frank**

Hey, Logic.

*[And the growls confused for guns are left unslung.]*

**Agnostic**

All right, we've asserted who's dominant. You are not... It's okay.

**Neighbor**

Settle, Frank. The dog is not a threat to you.

**Frank**

What's new on the end of your leash?

**Logic**

New food. The Lady Human is feeding me vegan-friendly options.

**Frank**

Oh, these two have gone full carnivore. It smells delicious. I'm still getting those kibbles and bits.

**Logic**

Oh, yeah. He's already planning his first grill party for when she goes to her mom's on Saturday.

**Agnostic**

These two are just arch nemeses.

**Neighbor**

It's funny trying to rationalize with an animal.

**Agnostic**

Ha, yeah, that's why I named the dog Logic. It's only felt halfway correct. Bright little pup, but she's a total only child.

**Logic**

Any news worth passing on your yellow stream?

**Frank**

You know Tim, right?

**Logic**

The Yorkie, two streets south.

**Frank**

No! A pitbull, three streets west.

**Logic**

Ohhhh! Totally! Tim's a real stand-up b\*\*ch.

**Frank**

Yeah! Just went to the farm upstate.

**Logic**

Good for Tim. Tim deserves the vacation.

**Agnostic**

Can you believe these two?

**Neighbor**

Yeah, totally not conducive to conversation.

**Agnostic**

I know, right? Way to ruin the conversation, dog.

**Frank**

Can you believe these two?

**Logic**

They always think we're enemies, right?

**Frank**

So ridiculous. Good to see you, Logic.

**Logic**

You too, Frank. Congrats on your pregnancy. How many are you expecting?

**Frank**

So many. I'm getting all these free belly scratches off the ground.

**Logic**

I'm a lifelong, lone pup over here.

**Frank**

Oh, I didn't know. I'm so jealous. You must sleep whenever you want.

**Logic**

I do. It's, um, crazy! I control the house.

**Agnostic**

You must admit, that dog can take you! Settle.

Stop! Stop! No!

**Frank**

So rude.

**Logic**

Right? Why don't they understand that "dog" is intense? Like German.

**Frank**

Uh, what's that Star Trek language from the dude-heavy forehead wrinkles?

**Logic**

Klingons. So few women in that species, right?

**Frank**

Yeah, Klingons. We're totally the inspiration for Klingons. Someone must have broke the oath with that guy Roddenberry.

**Logic**

Alright, I gotta s\*\*t.

**Frank**

So, should we placate them or make them drag us off talking?

**Logic**

Placate, I really need to sniff two streets over.

**Frank**

Get it!

*[Are pups immediately cease their barking and drag their god-captors in opposite directions.]*

**Agnostic**

There we go. Thank you for listening.

**Frank**

Nice training.

**Agnostic**

Yeah, and you too.

*[Doggy side is immediate.]*
