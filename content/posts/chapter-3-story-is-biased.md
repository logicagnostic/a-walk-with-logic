INT. LARGE COFFEE SHOP - LATE AFTERNOON

The drums hit hard, kick drum first. The crowd is hot. The fingers of the organist flicker down the keyboard.

**Deacon Denial**

Welcome, welcome. Welcome to the stories of life.

*(A chuckle breaks through. Our drummer and choir begin a pattern of claps, snaps and slaps across their chests as Deacon slides up to the microphone.)*

The first prank on humanity. I'm Brother Deacon Denial. Praise be to you.

*(Our crowd whistles, hoots and hollars. The organist trills the keys)*

The world is guided by story. What does that mean? Well, every day we're writing the story of humankind. Whether it's our own story or the people around us, we're telling stories to keep ourselves sane. We, the people, understand life through the lens of a journey. A commonly known journey is the hero's journey. This will be discussed in future chapters. But for now, the story is what we tell ourselves to understand the unexplained. Story is what we use to bring excitement to the moments between the mundane.

Just as words are made to evolve, story is a human-made tool for exploration, entertainment, and information gathering. In the simplest terms, let's face the fact that the human story is edited to fit the writer’s needs. Whether it's non-fiction or fiction, reality or reality TV, we've allied with allegory. We piece together stories to spread the word to those near and far.

But humans have yet to master this skill. It's a game of telephone and the call keeps cutting out.

*(Telephone rings)*

**Creepy kids in the audience**

Hello *(echos)*

**Adults in the audience**

Hey! Hey!

**Deacon Denial**

Metaphor, and simile. Allegory… Characters, plots, Conflict, theme, setting, and narrative. These are not precise tools. Evolution takes time and our eyes open slowly to the morning light.

*(A symphonic crescendo hits and holds as the clapping returns. The organist returns to trill.)*

This is even hypocritical of me as a storyteller telling you a story of my own bias. We've defined humanity by its story of the past. An inaccurate narrator. We… We have an incomplete picture of life.

Now, these tools of the trade have been limited to a select few voices over thousands of years by the commerce of power. The Bible, the silver screens, big and small. These stories barely scratch the surface of the human experience and more often lack the true story of humankind.

*(The drums erupt, splashing across the toms, snare, and cymbals with our organist. Our crowd erupts.)*

So, take our words and the words of everyone with a grain of salt. Whether it's an oral tradition or modern content, story is edited to fit the needs of the writer or the want of money. The story is messy. Like a child's room and they asking for a whooping.

You know what I am saying.

Society is not ready to exist without story as it stands today.

**A small child from the crowd**

What do you mean, man?

**Deacon Denial**

Shut up!

We're closer than ever to expressing honest moments. Or at least I hope so. Ya know with the internet, we opened the floodgates of the true human experience. Inside the true human mind. We can now connect to our neighbors worldwide. We're closer than ever to understanding the lives of the commoner. But we must stop relying on the mechanisms of story currently in place, taught to us by history. Biased by history.

With connection comes a realization that many of the stories we know are missing pieces. The powerful planted the binary seeds of heroes and villains.

*(A villainous multinote crescendo hits.)*

We’ll break that down in our final chapter this season. But it's imperative, we accept that the mechanisms of story must evolve.

**Crowd**

Yes! Yes! Yes. Yep yep!

**Deacon Denial**

And to do so we'll need to fathom that our history was written with a bias not favoring the commoner and that the tools used to tell these stories are not working for the commoner.

So help me out. When I say story. You say?

**Crowd**

Biased. Yep

*(Our band slips into fills and drums hit at the direction of Deacon with his crowd interactions)*

**Deacon Denial**

History?

**Crowd**

Biased. Yep!

**Deacon Denial**

Story of the powerful?

**Crowd**

Biased. Yep! Biased. Yep! Biased. Yep!

**Deacon Denial**

And what of the story of the people?

**Crowd**

Biased. Yep!

**Deacon Denial**

Seems pretty logical to us. Story is a tool we can use better. Let's stop wasting too much time believing the biased history.

If the stories are filled with absolutes, duality, heroes, and villains, it’s biased.

Let's consider life's spectrum of humanity is the rainbow that we've just begun to see.

*(Our crowd hollers with joy!)*

Join us next week for part one of the three-part, hypothetical journey through humanity's evolution. Praise be to you. Praise be to humanity. Praise be to humanity.

**Crowd**

Praise be to humanity. Praise be! Praise be!
