INT. TODAY'S TEMPORARY CHURCH IN THE AGNOSTIC MOUNT - EVENING

Our crowd is standing. Their chairs folded and laid on the ground. They are small, quiet but focused on the words.

The Deacon stands close to the microphone and pulls it closely. His hand points to the percussionist who kicks off the ethnic drive of the congas and cymbals.

**Deacon Denial**

Welcome to another sermon on the agnostic mount. We are Brother Deacon Denial. Today, we switch our focus. For the past three chapters, we've discussed the mechanisms of a binary world. Today, we want to take a journey to a time before Jesus and Zeus walked the same corners. A time when we the people, our ancestors, lived simple yet violent lives as hunter-gatherers, tents, and cave dwellers.

It was at this time that change was in the air. Humanity began to organize a civilization by finding communal goals. Small tribes connect, emerge from their hidden corners, to form larger communal organisms.

It's also at this time… At this moment, an extra-planetary being noticed our evolution. These EPs studied our growth. They saw great potential in humanity’s evolution, in our people. Drop whatever, man-made fear of aliens you’ve cultivated.

They saw *us*, stressed-out animals living on pure survival and adrenaline… And wanted to help. They sought to connect and educate.

Now, consider these EPs to be a positive force with good intentions. Perhaps they saw a chance at friendship in a lonely galaxy. There may have been no Star Trek prime directive in place.

*(The Deacon chuckles)*

Y'all know what I'm talking about.

So… for whatever their reason. They made their presence known to our ancestors. Like a realtor to the stars, our extra-planetary friends showed us the wonders of the pyramids. Our friends told the tale of air-conditioned, close-knit communities. They spun tales of safety… of family campfires with kids, freedom from harsh climates, and more.

These EPs sought to bring us their logical HOA in housing big enough for swaths of humanity.

These EPs sought to teach us to fish, not just hand us a fish. So, they directed our focus on learning the keys to building pyramids, promising a simpler life now focused on thriving rather than traumatic surviving.

And with this, they brought us education, organization, mathematics, and clearer language skills. All centered on increasing our mutual understanding to achieve the benefits of these communes. They gave people the tools needed to go forth and spread this knowledge.

Now inadvertently, this led us to the first steps toward three-dimensionalizing humanity. And much like every origin story, it must have been paradise for a period of time.

This led to bringing the mass majority together for a common goal, with community benefit at its centerpoint.

Even during construction, the equality of the people must have been ideal. These pyramids not only gave us our first chapter at proper shelter but also the chance to think bigger and expand our minds.

*(The Deacon’s voice suddenly resonates in the final words of that sentence.)*

Certainly, we've imagined what paradise must be like. Certainly, we've imagined what paradise might be. Let's play make-believe like we did as children.

Shout it out. What would it take for life to be a paradise for you?

**Crowd**

No taxes. No money. A better job. Safety!

**Deacon Denial**

Those words feel artificial for the people. Those words are fear-based. As we discussed. Words are made up and must evolve. There are better words out there we can use. Perhaps, something communal? Something that benefits human-kind?

**Crowd**

Connections. Understanding, Friendship. Just being me.

**Deacon Denial**

Okay… *(the Deacon is excited by this response.)* That's it. Our words must build community today as the pyramids must have in times long ago. Humanity got access to the catalyst of evolution: knowledge.

Our intergalactic friends gave the people what they lacked: the tools to build a community of love… Education elevated the people… And with freedom of thought, individuality served a greater good.

Their influence played a key role in our evolution on a global level.

Suddenly, education drives empathy in the working class. A deceleration of violence and minimized tribal warfare. Humanity starts to see the world in three dimensions.

Now, as history explains in every oral tradition, storybook, movie, and more, our paradise was not meant to last. Our extra-planetary friends were ultimately taught a lesson:

You can lead a horse to water. You can't stop it from pooping in that very same water.

*(This line gives the Deacon a moment of guffaw.)*

Next week's sermon on the agnostic mount explores how humanity shat where it ate. It ain't the story of Eve, a snake, and an apple. No, no, no.

We discuss why a prime directive is impossible and necessary for our Extra-Planetary friends. They find exactly what one expects: greed, violence, and marketing.

*(Deacon chuckles.)* That’s right, I said it: marketing.

I suppose good deeds never go unpunished. *(Shivers)* Help me. Help you. Help me. Help you. Help us all end this rather dreary sermon on a happier note with some music. My friends in the band? Play us a happy song, please.

*(The band switches from the rhythmic pattern to a swinging tune driven by the organist.)*

Praise be to you, my friends. Until next week, praise be to you. Praise be the people. Praise be the people.

*(The crowd remains silent. Their mouths open and are shocked.)*
