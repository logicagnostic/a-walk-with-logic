I like people in small groups. Much like
George Carlin. I like people in small groups.
The larger you get,
The more difficult it is to avoid mob mentality.
The larger you get,
The more likely the loudest voice is heard
And not the most rational,
And not the most researched,
And not the most willing
To accept doubt or the most willing to say
they don't know.
Large groups. Absolutes.
Small groups are willing to consider
that there is not always clear right or wrong,
But there are shades
Of colors.
Unlike people in large groups.
